The Quiet Power of Being Yourself

Whether you are looking for love or friendship, the best method comes down to three words: be yourself.

Express your values. Let your real life show. Then wait for people who resonate with that truth to come closer. Do not keep calculating what to say or how to act so that someone will like you. Living that way makes you false, tired, and deeply depleted.

Even if you do attract someone that way, you will still have to keep performing. Sooner or later, it will fall apart.

Being yourself has energy because it is honest.

And you do not need to be especially attractive to one specific person. Whoever's approval you crave becomes the person who owns you. Whether one person likes you or not is not as important as it seems.

People on your frequency will stay. The ones who stay are the right ones. Do not lose yourself for anyone.
