Protect Your Freedom to Build

People who truly want to build something significant would often rather be a little poorer and a little less occupied during a low period than trap themselves for years in work that demands constant intensity while offering little freedom.

It is not that they cannot endure hardship. Meaningful work requires long stretches of solitude, thought, and trial and error. When someone is exhausted every day and can think only about sleep, the mind has very little left to give.

When creating a new product, first study how the competitors work. Why did they make those choices? What does a good experience feel like? Do not begin with self directed fantasies and assumptions. Understand the strongest competitors first, identify what makes their experience work, and then improve it with a clear difference of your own.
