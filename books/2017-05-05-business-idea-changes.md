# Your Business Idea Will Change. Here's How to Deal With It

**ID:** `2017-05-05-business-idea-changes`  
**Published:** May 5, 2017  
**Source:** https://www.inc.com/magazine/201705/norm-brodsky/business-idea-changes.html

## Bilingual note

**English:** New businesses rarely follow their original plans. Obstacles and unexpected opportunities force founders to rethink the model, so the job is to respond to evidence without losing sight of the underlying goal.

**中文：** 新企业很少能完全按照最初的计划发展。障碍和意外机会会迫使创业者重新思考商业模式，因此真正重要的是根据证据调整，同时不要丢掉底层目标。
