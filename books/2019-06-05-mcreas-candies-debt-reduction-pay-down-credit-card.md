# How This Caramel Company Escaped Credit Card Debt With 1 Simple Strategy

**ID:** `2019-06-05-mcreas-candies-debt-reduction-pay-down-credit-card`  
**Published:** June 5, 2019  
**Source:** https://www.inc.com/magazine/201906/norm-brodsky/mcreas-candies-debt-reduction-pay-down-credit-card.html

## Bilingual note

**English:** A growing caramel company used several zero-interest credit cards, then faced expensive balances when sales slowed. Brodsky's advice was to pay the minimum on every card except the one with the highest interest rate, and direct all extra cash to that balance before moving to the next one.

**中文：** 一家快速增长的焦糖公司曾使用多张零利率信用卡融资，但销售放缓后，未偿余额开始产生高额利息。Brodsky 建议：除利率最高的卡外，其他卡只偿还最低还款额，把所有额外现金集中用于清偿最高利率债务，再依次处理下一张卡。
