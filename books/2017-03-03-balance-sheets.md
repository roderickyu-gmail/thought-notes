# Why It Made Sense for This Entrepreneur to Give Her Inventory Away

**ID:** `2017-03-03-balance-sheets`  
**Published:** March 3, 2017  
**Source:** https://www.inc.com/magazine/201703/norm-brodsky/balance-sheets.html

## Bilingual note

**English:** A balance sheet can reveal problems that an income statement hides. Excess inventory ties up cash and warehouse space, so understanding the balance sheet helps an owner make difficult but economically rational decisions.

**中文：** 资产负债表能揭示利润表掩盖的问题。过多库存会占用现金和仓储空间，因此理解资产负债表，才能帮助企业主做出艰难但符合经济规律的决定。
