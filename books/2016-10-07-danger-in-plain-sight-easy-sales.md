# Don't Make This Dangerous (but Surprisingly Common) Sales Mistake

**ID:** `2016-10-07-danger-in-plain-sight-easy-sales`  
**Published:** October 7, 2016  
**Source:** https://www.inc.com/magazine/201610/norm-brodsky/danger-in-plain-sight-easy-sales.html

## Bilingual note

**English:** Easy sales can create concentration risk. If most revenue comes from one customer, owner, industry, or network, a single policy change or relationship failure can damage the whole company and reduce its value.

**中文：** 容易拿下的订单也可能制造客户集中风险。如果大部分收入来自同一个客户、所有者、行业或关系网络，一次政策变化或关系破裂，就可能重创整个公司，并降低它的价值。
