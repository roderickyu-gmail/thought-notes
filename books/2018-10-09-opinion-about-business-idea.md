# Got a New Business Idea? Don't Ask Anyone Else's Opinion

**ID:** `2018-10-09-opinion-about-business-idea`  
**Published:** October 9, 2018  
**Source:** https://www.inc.com/magazine/201810/norm-brodsky/opinion-about-business-idea.html

## Bilingual note

**English:** The article argues that an entrepreneur should not outsource conviction to other people's opinions. Outside feedback can be useful, but the essential question is whether the founder understands and believes in the idea well enough to test it.

**中文：** 文章认为，创业者不应该把做决定的底气外包给别人的意见。外部反馈当然有价值，但更关键的问题是：创业者是否真正理解这个想法，并且有足够信心把它拿去验证。
