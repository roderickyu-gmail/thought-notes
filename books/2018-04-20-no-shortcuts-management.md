# Why Building a Company to Last and Building a Company to Sell Are the Same Thing

**ID:** `2018-04-20-no-shortcuts-management`  
**Published:** April 20, 2018  
**Source:** https://www.inc.com/magazine/201805/norm-brodsky/no-shortcuts-management.html

## Bilingual note

**English:** Build for the long term while keeping the business healthy enough to sell. Both goals require sound systems, ethical decisions, durable customer relationships, and resistance to shortcuts that create hidden liabilities.

**中文：** 既要按长期经营的标准建设公司，也要让公司保持随时可以出售的健康状态。这两个目标都要求建立可靠的系统、做出合乎道德的决策、维护持久的客户关系，并抵制那些会制造隐性负债的捷径。
