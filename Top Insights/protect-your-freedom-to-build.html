Protect Your Freedom to Build

People who truly want to build something significant would often rather be a little poorer and a little less occupied during a low period than trap themselves for years in work that demands constant intensity while offering little freedom.

It is not that they cannot endure hardship. Meaningful work requires solitude, thought, and trial and error. When someone is exhausted every day and can think only about sleep, the mind has very little left to give.

When creating a new product, study the competitors first. Understand why they made their choices and what makes a good experience work. Build from that understanding, then add your own improvements and difference.
