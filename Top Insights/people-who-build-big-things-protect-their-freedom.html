People Who Build Big Things Protect Their Freedom

People who truly want to do something significant would often rather be a little poorer and a little less occupied during a low period than trap themselves for years in work that demands constant intensity while offering little freedom.

It is not that they cannot endure hardship. It is that meaningful work requires long stretches of solitude, thought, and trial and error. When someone is exhausted every day and can think only about sleep, the mind has very little left to give.
